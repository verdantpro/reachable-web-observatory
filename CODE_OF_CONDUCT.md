# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Project spaces should be respectful, technically rigorous, and safe for people
of every background and level of experience. Constructive disagreement is
welcome; harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct,
deliberate disruption, and publication of private or sensitive data are not.

Research discussion must also respect the Observatory's ethical boundaries.
Do not use project spaces to coordinate exploitation of observed hosts or to
publish details that make a third party easier to attack.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `research@verdantprotocol.com`. Security
issues and sensitive host data should instead go to
`abuse@verdantprotocol.com`. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject
contributions, restrict participation, or ban participants when necessary.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a
good-faith reporter is prohibited.
