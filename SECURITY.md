# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for a vulnerability in the Observatory,
collector, scanner, deployment configuration, or data-access controls.

Use GitHub's private vulnerability-reporting form when it is enabled for this
repository, or email `abuse@verdantprotocol.com`. Include the affected component,
reproduction steps, impact, and any suggested mitigation. Encryption is not
currently required; avoid including unrelated personal data or credentials.

## Response targets

- Acknowledgement within two days.
- Initial triage within seven days for issues affecting the Observatory itself.
- Material-status updates, or an update at least every 14 days during an active
  investigation.
- Record correction and removal requests targeted within two days through the
  separate operator workflow.

These are response targets, not guarantees of resolution within those periods.
The public coordinated-disclosure policy is available at
https://observatory.verdantprotocol.com/disclosure.

## Scope and safe harbor

Good-faith testing that stays within the project's published ethical boundaries
is welcome. Do not exploit observed third-party hosts, access non-public data,
degrade service, persist access, or publish sensitive findings before remediation.
We will not pursue action against good-faith reporters who respect these limits.

The canonical machine-readable contact file is
https://observatory.verdantprotocol.com/.well-known/security.txt.
